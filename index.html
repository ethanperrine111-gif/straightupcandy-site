Why we started

Candy shouldn't need a decoder ring.

Most candy is built from a handful of industrial inputs dressed up with color and flavor. We flipped that: start with real, whole-food ingredients, and build the candy around them instead of the other way around.

No corn syrup, no artificial dyes, no ingredients you need a chemistry degree to understand. Just candy, made straight up.

  • Whole-food, recognizable ingredients
  • No artificial colors or flavors
  • Made for adults who still love candy
The difference

What "straight up" actually means

We're not vague about it. Here's exactly what's different.

Typical candy

  • Corn syrup as the base
  • Artificial colors & dyes
  • Long, unrecognizable ingredient lists
  • Flavor built in a lab

Straight Up Candy

  • Whole-food ingredients, real sugar
  • Color from real fruit & plants
  • Short, plain-English labels
  • Flavor from the ingredients themselves
